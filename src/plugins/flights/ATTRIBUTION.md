# Aircraft artwork attribution

The side-profile aircraft art in `render/aircraft/` is derived from technical
drawings published on Wikimedia Commons. Each source drawing was cropped to its
SIDE elevation, stroke-thickened and reduced to 1-bit black on white for the
e-ink panel by `tools/build_cc_aircraft.py`.

**These derived images inherit the CC BY-SA 3.0 licence of their sources.**
Share-alike applies: if you redistribute them, modified or not, they must stay
under CC BY-SA 3.0 with the credit below. This is independent of the licence
covering the rest of this repository's code.
