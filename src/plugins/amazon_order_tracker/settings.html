Amazon Order Tracker

Minimum 5 minutes

🔐 Gmail Connection

Not your regular Gmail password. Generate one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords (needs 2-Step Verification turned on first). This never expires until you revoke it - no Google Cloud project, no re-authenticating every week.

ℹ️ How It Works

  • Parses Amazon order emails from your Gmail via IMAP
  • Extracts order #, product, status, delivery date
  • Detects "Arriving today" straight from shipping emails, not just the Out-for-delivery one
  • Displays filtered by mode (today, in-transit, etc)
  • App password stored only on your Pi, in plugin settings
